The valid intents are the following:
- Account: Managing the user's account, identity, and security.
- Cards: Obtaining, activating, and troubleshooting cards.
- Disputes: Raising a formal issue about a transaction.
- Payments: Making purchases, withdrawals, or currency exchanges.
- Topups: Loading money onto the user's own balance.
- Transfers: Moving money between the user and another party.
